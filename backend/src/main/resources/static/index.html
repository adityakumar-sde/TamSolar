Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At TAM Solar Solutions, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you enquire about our solar products and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Your full name and contact information (mobile number, email address).
  • Property type (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Trade Partner).
  • Solar requirements, estimated electricity usage, and roof details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Prepare accurate solar quotes, system sizing, and ROI estimates.
  • Schedule site feasibility assessments and answer technical queries.
  • Provide post-installation maintenance and warranty support.

3. No Spam & No Third-Party Sales

We respect your time and privacy. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party telemarketers or advertisers. You will only be contacted by TAM Solar Solutions specialists regarding your specific enquiry.

4. Data Security

All submitted enquiries are stored in secure, encrypted databases protected by strict industry-standard access controls, firewalls, and token-based administrative authentication.

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to TAM Solar Solutions. By browsing this website, requesting quotes, or partnering with us, you agree to the following terms and guidelines.

1. Quotations & System Estimates

Any online estimations, calculator outputs, or preliminary quotes are indicative and based on standard sunlight hours and average tariffs. Final pricing and system capacity are confirmed following an on-site physical roof inspection and shadow analysis.

2. Tier-1 Equipment & Warranties

TAM Solar Solutions supplies Tier-1 solar modules, certified on-grid/hybrid inverters, and high-tensile mounting structures. Product warranties (typically 25-year performance warranty for solar panels and 5-10 year manufacturer warranty for inverters) are backed by the respective original equipment manufacturers.

3. Installation & Commissioning

Installations are executed by certified solar engineers adhering to MNRE (Ministry of New and Renewable Energy) standards and local DISCOM grid interconnection safety regulations.

4. Customer Responsibilities

The customer is responsible for providing safe roof access, necessary civil clearances, and required electricity bill documentation for net metering applications with the local DISCOM.
